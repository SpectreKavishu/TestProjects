lightbulb Tactical Pro Tips

Tip 01

The Power of Silence

After receiving an offer, wait 3-5 seconds. This non-confrontational silence often prompts recruiters to clarify or soften their stance immediately.

Tip 02

Focus on Total Value

Don't fixate on base salary. Equity, signing bonuses, and professional development budgets are often more flexible levers.

Tip 03

Data is Objective

Frame requests around "market alignment" rather than personal "need." It depersonalizes the request and makes it professional.
